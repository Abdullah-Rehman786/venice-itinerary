Two At-Home Date Nights

Monday • Date Night #2

Cozy poolside wind-down
  • Keep this one more relaxed and effortless.

Date-night rules for this trip

🏠 Stay at the villa while the kids are sleeping.

🚫 Do not assume the Airbnb provides decorations or hosting extras.

🤍 Simple > elaborate. The point is ease, not effort.
